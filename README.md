## Hi, I'm Rafael 👋

A **Professor**, **Software Engineer** and **Tech Entrepreneur** based in Itatiba, São Paulo, Brazil. I teach Technology and Computing at **FATEC Jundiaí (Centro Paula Souza)**, co-founded **Schuster & Gross Tecnologia**, and I'm currently pursuing my **PhD**.

For more than five years I've been splitting my time between the classroom and production: designing and running real systems for events, condominiums and education, managing my own infrastructure, and helping students turn ideas into working software.

<br clear="right"/>

---

### 🚀 What I Do

- **Teaching & Mentoring:** Algorithms and Programming Logic, Operating Systems (Linux), Databases, Software Engineering and Interdisciplinary Projects. I coordinate team based projects where students ship real products every two weeks.
- **Product Building:** Full cycle development of SaaS platforms, from data modeling to payments, push notifications and deployment.
- **Infrastructure & Security:** Self-hosted servers, virtualization, firewalls and hardening. Proxmox clusters, pfSense gateways with failover, Linux VPS, mail servers and Docker.
- **Research:** Bibliometric analysis, blockchain beyond cryptocurrencies, and tools that help researchers work faster.
